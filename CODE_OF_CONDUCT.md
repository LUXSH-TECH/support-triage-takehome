# Code of conduct

We want the take-home process to be respectful and low-stress for everyone involved.

## Our expectations

- Be respectful and professional in issues, pull requests, and the live review.
- Submit your own work. Using AI coding assistants is allowed and expected — just be honest
  about where and how you used them, and make sure you understand and can modify what you
  submit.
- Do not share the assignment, fixtures, or other candidates' submissions outside the
  hiring process.
- Assume good faith. If something in the assignment is unclear, ask or state a reasonable
  assumption and move on.

## Scope and contact

This applies to all interactions in this repository and during the review. To raise a
concern about someone's conduct, contact your Luxsh hiring contact, or
`hiran.harilal@luxshtech.com`.

Adapted, in spirit, from the Contributor Covenant.
